## Corpus-level review

Result: accepted as stronger external-validity witness evidence, with bounded
wording.

Executed evidence:

- Unit count: 5.
- Repository or independent subsystem count: 4.
- Typed verdict counts: 5 pass.
- Minimum success checks in the summary report: all true.

Allowed conclusion:

- The paper now has a small external issue/PR/commit-linked semantic witness
  corpus across DeepXDE, NeuralOperator, PhiFlow/PhiML, and JAX-CFD.
- The corpus directly weakens the objection that the method only works on
  self-made cylinder-flow tasks.

Forbidden conclusion:

- It is not production CFD validation.
- It is not trained-SUT correctness evidence.
- It is not a representative sample of SciML defects.
- It is not a real-world defect-detection rate.
- It is not broad framework correctness evidence for DeepXDE, NeuralOperator,
  PhiFlow, PhiML, or JAX-CFD.

Theme-drift decision:

- No drift for the accepted wording. The corpus remains within the paper's
  topic because each unit is framed as an MR-card / source-follow-up / metric /
  typed-verdict semantic witness.
- It would drift if promoted to a bug-mining study, a framework-quality survey,
  or production reliability evidence.
